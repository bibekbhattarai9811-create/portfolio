Overview

The idea

I wanted help growing a side income without doing every step myself: researching topics, drafting titles and posts, and keeping track of what happened yesterday.

AI Office treats that as a team. Six agents with distinct roles work through a daily cycle, now focused on growing my two YouTube channels, and a hard rule keeps me in charge: agents prepare, I send.

Approach

How a day works

  1. The CEO plans

    Ada, the CEO agent, reads the mission, the last three reports and my approve/reject decisions, then assigns three to six tasks.

  2. The team works in order

    Rex researches with live web search and cites sources, Nia analyses, Mia writes titles, posts and SEO, Bo writes specs and Otto handles operations. Each agent sees what earlier teammates produced.

  3. Nothing leaves without me

    Posts, emails, DMs, sign-ups and any spending land in an Approvals tab. New videos from VideoForge arrive there for review too.

  4. A report that feeds tomorrow

    The CEO writes a daily report ending in a NEXT GOAL, and a morning brief is pushed to my phone.

Trading desk

A paper-trading desk

I added an 11-role trading team (chart scanner, news monitor, entry timer, exit planner, risk manager, budget manager, order executor and more) that trades a $1,000 paper account, never real money.

Setups are scored on VWAP, moving averages, RSI, volume and nearness to the session high, with 1% risk per trade, a daily loss stop, at most two open positions and no trades near earnings. A trading journal writes a daily entry and a weekly scorecard.

A Strategy Lab backtests rule sets across 12 symbols. The honest first result: over 60 days, every rule set lost money. Stocks came out roughly flat, and on crypto the fees alone (about 0.8% per round trip) were as large as the whole stop distance. That evidence now drives which markets the desk is allowed to trade.

Decisions

Key decisions

Every agent has a fallback chain

Each agent tries its preferred model first and falls through Claude, Gemini, Groq and NVIDIA. Free models get retired often, so every runner walks a list of models.

Runs itself every morning

A scheduled task runs the cycle at 8 AM, catches up when the PC wakes, runs at most once per day, and gives every step a deadline so one hung web search can't block the day.

Paper only, research framing

The desk can't place real trades, and its output is labelled as research, not financial advice.

Make the work visible

A cartoon 3D office shows the agents at their desks and the traders on a trading floor with live screens, and it can push trades and alerts to my phone.
