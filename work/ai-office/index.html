Overview

The idea

I wanted help building a side income without doing every step myself: researching niches, drafting outreach, writing posts and keeping track of what happened yesterday.

AI Office treats that as a team. Six agents with distinct roles work through a daily cycle, and a hard rule keeps me in charge: agents prepare, I send.

Approach

How a day works

  1. The CEO plans

    Ada, the CEO agent, reads the mission, the last three reports and my approve/reject decisions, then assigns three to six tasks.

  2. The team works in order

    Rex researches with live web search and cites sources, Nia analyses, Mia writes marketing, Bo writes specs and Otto handles operations. Each agent can see what earlier teammates produced.

  3. Nothing leaves without me

    Posts, emails, DMs, sign-ups and any spending land in an Approvals tab. Nothing is ever sent automatically.

  4. A report that feeds tomorrow

    The CEO writes a daily report ending in a NEXT GOAL, which becomes the starting point of the next cycle.

Decisions

Key decisions

Every agent has a fallback chain

Each agent tries its preferred model first and falls through Claude, Gemini, Groq and NVIDIA. Free models get retired often, so every runner walks a list of models instead of hard-coding one.

Runs itself every morning

A scheduled task runs the cycle at 8 AM, catches up when the PC wakes or I log in, runs at most once per day, and gives every step a deadline so one hung web search can't block the day.

Human approval as a feature

The approval queue isn't a limitation, it's the product. It makes it safe to let agents draft real outreach.

Make the work visible

A cartoon 3D office shows the agents at their desks, walking to the CEO when a task is done and sending documents to my inbox when something needs approval.
